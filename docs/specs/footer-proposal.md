# Footer and proposal dialog

## Footer

The contact CTA band starts with a deep-purple `Let's Connect` panel and a pale contact panel. The lower footer is near-black purple and includes the complete live service list, company links, selected industries, `Have a great idea? Let’s Chat`, directory trust badge, partner badges, social links, copyright, Privacy Policy, and Terms & Conditions. It is multi-column on desktop and stacked on mobile.

## Proposal dialog

Accessible client-side dialog matching the captured `Get My Proposal` panel. It displays a progress bar with 25/50/75/100 labels, preserves entered state across Previous/Next, validates required controls, dismisses with close/Escape/backdrop, and prevents background scroll. Final action creates a prefilled email to `info@codezela.com`; no fake success or unavailable API is introduced.
